### **Beyond Cacao**

Surya Cacao is beyond ceremonial-grade cacao.

We're a community driving the expansion of third spaces, spaces that rebel against constant performance, optimization, and efficiency, and instead make room for people to genuinely bring their cortisol down, elevate their mood, and befriend rather than network.

Every generation has chosen its own drinking _ritual_. Rituals are the essence of human nature: your morning coffee, your post-workout matcha, your evening cocktail are all a version of the same instinct. Our mission is to build a new generation of drinking _ritual_, one that steps back into ancestral tradition and brings back the drink of the Gods, a drink that supports your body, mind, and spirit, rather than breaking them down.

Surya Cacao is made by three families across three continents, coming together to share what we've had the privilege of enjoying for years: sourced from our partners, a Dominican family in the Samaná region of the Dominican Republic; made by Polish immigrants on the island; and shared globally by Indian natives, so it can reach your cup, and your home.

Surya exists to offer the beauty of heart-opening cacao without gatekept spiritual practice. _Because your morning cup is Your Ceremony._

### **About Ceremonial Cacao**

Ceremonial cacao is a distinct kind of cacao — unique in how it's fermented and produced, with minimal processing so it retains its full chemical composition.

Rich in **theobromine**, it offers an elevated mood and steady energy that carries through the day, while supporting cognitive clarity and cardiovascular health.

Naturally low in caffeine, it doesn't spike cortisol — it soothes it. Ceremonial cacao is also one of the richest natural sources of **magnesium** in the world, alongside meaningful amounts of **iron, potassium, zinc, manganese, and copper**, plus the antioxidant flavanols cacao is prized for.

At Surya, we pledge to never compromise the quality of our cacao — so that each piece gives you peace. {{Add a CTA}}
