⚠ NOT DRAFTED — intentionally.

This page needs real text from someone qualified to write it. A privacy policy
is a binding legal statement about what you collect and why; plausible-sounding
placeholder prose is worse than an empty page, because it reads as finished and
can ship unreviewed.

Whoever writes it will need to cover, at minimum: what the Shopify checkout
collects, what the newsletter and inquiry forms store and where, any analytics,
cookies, the lawful basis for EU visitors under GDPR, and how someone requests
deletion.

**Do not launch with this page as-is.**
