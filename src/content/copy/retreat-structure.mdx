Set in the Samaná region, surrounded by turquoise sea and jungle, Surya Center
is an ecological facility — built to hold space, not perform luxury. All food on
site is grown in collaboration with local farmers: pesticide-free, GMO-free, and
truly nourishing. A real farm-to-table experience, from the same land your cacao
comes from.

As part of your retreat, we'll also bring you to our ceremonial cacao farm,
where you can see the source firsthand and try the variety of beans for
yourself.
