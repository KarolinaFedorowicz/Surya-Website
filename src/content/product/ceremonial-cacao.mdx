above are fixed by §2.3 and must not be renamed.

Three of the five carry real copy from the copy source:

- `origin` — the three-families sourcing line from Beyond Cacao, plus the
  organic line from Who We Are.

  feeds C4 RitualQuantity, replacing the framings that were hardcoded there.

⚠ TWO FIELDS ARE NOT DRAFTED, deliberately:

- `tasting_notes` — the copy source contains no sensory description anywhere.
  A tasting note is a claim about how the product actually tastes; inventing one
  is the kind of placeholder that ships unreviewed because it reads as finished.
- `weight_note` — servings per size and prep guidance were not supplied. The
  source gives weights and prices only, and the recipes that would establish a
  dose are themselves an open item (see recipes-intro.mdx).

Both render as absent rather than empty in their designed slots. `placeholder`
stays true until they are filled.

here. Price belongs to the commerce layer, and lib/mock/ and lib/shopify/ are
out of scope this pass. They will need setting against the real variants.
