---
title: The Long Table
mood: For when there are more of you than cups
ingredients:
  - 150g ceremonial cacao, chopped
  - 1.2l water
  - 2 cinnamon sticks
  - Orange peel, in wide strips
featured: false
order: 4
image: null
placeholder: true
method:
  - Bring the water, cinnamon and orange peel to a bare simmer, then rest it off the heat for ten minutes.
  - Return to a low heat, whisk in the cacao in three additions.
  - Pour from height into the cups so it aerates.
