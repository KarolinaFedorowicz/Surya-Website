---
title: Cardamom & Rose
mood: For the evening that wants to slow down
ingredients:
  - 25g ceremonial cacao, chopped
  - 200ml water or oat milk
  - 2 green cardamom pods, cracked
  - 1 tsp dried rose petals
  - Cinnamon and a single dried chilli
featured: true
order: 2
image: null
placeholder: true
method:
  - Steep the cardamom, rose and chilli in the hot liquid for four minutes.
  - Strain, then whisk in the cacao until glossy.
  - Grate cinnamon over the top before serving.
