---
title: The First Pour
mood: For the morning you decide not to rush
ingredients:
  - 25g ceremonial cacao, chopped
  - 200ml water, just off the boil
  - A pinch of flaky salt
featured: true
order: 1
image: null
placeholder: true
method:
  - Warm the cup while the water settles off the boil.
  - Whisk the cacao into a third of the water until it turns glossy.
  - Add the rest slowly, whisking, then finish with salt.
